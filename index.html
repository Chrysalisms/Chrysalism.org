Governance Model

Public Trust & Neutral Governance

Chrysalism functions strictly as a public benefit organization. All steering board meetings are logged, and license reviews are public.

The Vendor-Neutral Guarantee

Much like the Linux Foundation and Apache Software Foundation, Chrysalism separates open core security codebase control from commercial platform initiatives.

⚖️
Public Steering Committee Direct community-led voting on repository roadmap and standards compliance.
🔐
Irrevocable License Protection All code hosted is bound irrevocably under standard approved OSI licenses.
💎
Transparent Funding Model Operating capital is derived entirely from public donations, grants, and sponsors.

Chrysalism Foundation Charter

"To safeguard and scale standard digital defense mechanisms by offering top-tier, low-cost architectural advice and ensuring the continuous vendor-neutral development of secure utility programs."

Chrysalism operations comply fully with 501(c)(3) guidelines. No director or contributor may claim intellectual property monopolies on hosted repositories.
