Core Safeguards

Delivering Calm & Trust in Tech

Our foundation is structured to shield enterprise security and operational architecture from private equity acquisitions, sudden price increases, and volatile license pivots.

🧭

Compassionate Advisory Care

Navigating volatile technology shifts shouldn't compromise your operations. We offer direct, trusted guidance on cloud operations, cybersecurity posture, and ethical AI integration—listening to your constraints and planning without commercial vendor pressure.

🛡️

A Sovereign Security Shield

Sustaining robust, enterprise-grade open-source replacements for complex proprietary security software. We ensure your core digital defenses remain permanently unencumbered and under your direct control.

📜

An Unshakable Legal Haven

Chrysalism is legally chartered to lock in vendor-neutrality. Our codebases are irrevocably bound under standard approved OSI licenses, permanently protecting them from private equity acquisitions or forced commercial monetization.

Governance Board

Your Peace of Mind, Protected by Charter

Chrysalism functions strictly as a public benefit organization. All steering board operations and licensing reviews are fully transparent.

The Vendor-Neutral Guarantee

Chrysalism's governance steering model is built to ensure stability. Legally bound by non-profit guidelines, our steering council exists solely to maintain public utility.

⚖️
Public Steering Committee

Direct community-led voting on repository roadmaps, dependency coordinates, and core standards compliance.

🔐
Irrevocable License Protection

All source code hosted is bound irrevocably under approved open-source licenses, shielding it from sudden corporate enclosement.

💎
Transparent Funding Model

Capital is derived entirely from public donations, philanthropy grants, and neutral memberships. No venture influence.

Chrysalism Covenants Charter

"To safeguard and scale standard digital defense mechanisms by offering top-tier, low-cost architectural advice and ensuring the continuous vendor-neutral development of secure utility programs."

Chrysalism operations comply fully with 501(c)(3) guidelines. No director or contributor may claim intellectual property monopolies on hosted repositories.
